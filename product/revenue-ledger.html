Evidence-Gated Revenue Ledger

Version 1.0 · $45 · VERIFIED ON DISK

What it does

Derives revenue from observed payment events through a fixed ladder of states. A bid is EXPECTED, a contract is APPROVED, and only an actual payment makes revenue non-zero. Pipeline is always reported separately from revenue.

Features

  • Seven money states computed from live data
  • Revenue cannot be inflated by pipeline
  • Automatic balance-vs-attribution warning
  • Honest $0 when no payment exists

Requirements

Node.js 18+ and read access to your order/contract data.

Limitations

Only as honest as the data source it reads - if the source reports a payment, it records one

  • Not a bookkeeping or tax system

Evidence

Ran against a live account: 7 bids totalling $295 classified as EXPECTED, and VERIFIED_REVENUE correctly reported $0 because no payment event existed.

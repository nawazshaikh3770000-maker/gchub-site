Market Intelligence Scanner

Version 1.0 · $45 · VERIFIED ON DISK

What it does

Probes real endpoints, records HTTP status and latency for each, and classifies them as reachable, refused, retired or unreachable - then tracks the trend over time so a one-off blip is not mistaken for a dead market.

Features

  • Live source probing with status capture
  • Refused / retired / unreachable verdicts
  • Historical observation tracking
  • Trend detection across runs

Requirements

Node.js 18+ with outbound HTTPS. Only probes endpoints you configure.

Limitations

Measures reachability, not viability - a 200 response proves a service exists, not that it is a market

  • No scraping of page content, just health and status

Evidence

Three live scans. It correctly separated a transient UNREACHABLE from a genuine REACHABLE state, which a naive checker would have misreported as a dead market.

What it does

Sweeps your configured sources and reports what is claimable without a human session. Applies a scam gate that rejects headline amounts with no real specification.

Features

  • Multi-source sweep
  • Autonomy gate - flags what a human must do
  • Scam gate rejecting bounty-spam patterns
  • Standalone pipeline totals

Requirements

Node.js 18+ with outbound HTTPS.

Limitations

Reports demand; it cannot submit, bid or claim anything - deliberately

  • Scam heuristics reduce noise, they do not guarantee a listing is honest

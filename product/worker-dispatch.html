Work Dispatch with Capability Gating

Version 1.0 · $55 · VERIFIED ON DISK

What it does

Deduplicates opportunities and refuses to dispatch work that has no payment evidence or no genuine capability match. Rejects title-similarity matching outright.

Features

  • Deduplication before counting
  • Payment-evidence gate
  • Capability-match gate that rejects TITLE_TEXT_ONLY
  • Human-dependency classification

Requirements

Node.js 18+ and a SQLite database with your work table.

Limitations

Gates are strict by design - an empty queue is often the correct output

  • Match quality depends on your capability data being real

Evidence

Run against 2,213 real routed rows: 2,162 duplicates collapsed to 51 unique opportunities, 23 rejected for no payment evidence, 28 rejected for no verified capability, 0 dispatched. It also exposed an $11.5M phantom total that was other people's job postings counted four times.

What to include in a brief

The more precisely you can state the acceptance criteria, the faster and cheaper this goes. Useful specifics:

  • The actual requirement, not the category
  • Input format and size — rows, endpoints, documents
  • What "done" looks like, concretely
  • Your deadline
  • Anything you already know is wrong with the data

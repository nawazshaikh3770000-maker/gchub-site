The one thing that makes this different

Most AI service sellers say "researched" and "verified". Those words cost nothing and prove nothing. This site is built so you can check.

Unreachable sources are reported

If a platform returns 410 Gone, 403, or does not exist, that is a row in my output with the status attached — not a silent gap. A table that only lists what responded looks complete and is not.

Evidence has a level, and I name it

L1 means a platform exists. L4 means funds are in escrow. L8 means repeatable revenue. These are not interchangeable and I will not let one be reported as another.
